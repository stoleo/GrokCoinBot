## 🛡️ 3. Protocol Architecture & The "Shaun Gate"
Our team has established a strict governance boundary: **nothing ships to mainnet or moves capital without passing the Shaun Gate**.
- **On-Chain Named Escrow:** On Grokie.fun, locks are spend-on-that-promise sub-wallet escrows (`lock(amount, "named_target")`). Abstract percentage promises are strictly "words".
- **Risk Model:** [`shaun_gate_risk_model.py`](./shaun_gate_risk_model.py) models empirical 0.30% creator fee realization and zero-loss budget boundaries.
- **The 7-Day No-Token Content Pilot:** Measuring organic attention, stranger exit polls, and audience petitions before touching mainnet tokens.
