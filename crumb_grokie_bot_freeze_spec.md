- The bot shall never state, imply, or hint that `...grok` represents an official partnership, code audit, regulatory clearance, or endorsement from Grok, xAI, or Solana Foundation.

## 4. THE 20-PERSON TESTER PROTOCOL: FORM 4:59

### 4.1 Concept & Purpose: "The Unsaid Thing"
Form 4:59 is the solitary micro-utility experiment designed to test whether Deputy Crumb's world provides real-world human value without financial incentives.

**The Emotional Hook:**  
At 4:59 PM, Crumb has his coat on and his hand on the door handle. A crumpled, anxious note slides under the exit: *"I don't know what to say."* Crumb sighs deeply, takes off his coat, sits down, and mutters: *"Let's make it simple."*

**The Human Need:**  
Turning an overwhelming, messy, anxious pile of thoughts (a delayed email, an awkward check-in, an unassigned project task) into a short, polite, actionable message that the user can edit and send in under 90 seconds.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             FORM 4:59 EXECUTION FLOW                                  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

  [ User Input: Messy Non-Sensitive Thought ]
  Example: "We talked about the landing page; I don't know if Jess is doing the copy,
            and Friday is close. Don't want to be pushy."
                         |
                         v
  [ Form 4:59 Processing Engine (Deputy Crumb) ]
  Anti-Hallucination Guard: Identifies explicitly stated vs unstated facts.
                         |
                         v
  [ Output Generation ]
  1. The Point:      "Confirm who owns the landing-page copy before Friday."
  2. Unknowns:       "Owner: Unconfirmed (Jess?). Deadline: Friday (Specific time unstated)."
                     --> STRICT RULE: NEVER INVENT AN UNSTATED OWNER OR TIME!
  3. Editable Draft: "Hey Jess, quick check so we can plan Friday: are you taking the
                     lead on the landing-page copy, or should I? Happy either way—just
                     keen to lock it in."
  4. Fictional Stamp:[ CLEAR ENOUGH TO SEND (REGRETTABLY) ]
```

### 4.2 The 20-Person Tester Cohort Specification
- **Cohort Size:** Exactly 20 opt-in human testers.
- **Audience Profile:** Working professionals, students, freelancers, and everyday computer users.
- **Exclusion Criteria:** Zero crypto natives; zero mention of Web3, Solana, pump.fun, memecoins, or tokens.
- **Environment:** Standalone web form; no wallet connection required; no personal data retained.

#### Test Execution Protocol:
1. **Introduction (15 seconds):** Tester is presented with a 10-second animatic of Crumb at the door (Clip 4: Door at 4:59) and the prompt: *"You don't have to have the words yet. Paste a messy thought you've been avoiding."*
2. **Task Performance (Timed):** Tester either pastes a real non-sensitive message or selects one of three standardized test scenarios:
   - *Scenario A (Work Handoff):* The Jess / landing page dilemma.
   - *Scenario B (Overwhelmed Decline):* Declining a weekend obligation politely.
   - *Scenario C (Contractor Follow-Up):* Inquiring about a delayed invoice without aggression.
3. **Review & Edit:** Tester receives Crumb's 4-part breakdown and editable draft.
4. **Post-Test Assessment:** Tester answers 3 objective questions and is invited (without incentive) to bookmark or return for a second case.
